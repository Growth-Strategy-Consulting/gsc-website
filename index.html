What this is not

Not therapy

I am not a therapist and I do not treat trauma or mental health conditions. We can look at patterns and where they came from. If what you need is clinical care, I will tell you.

Not motivation

You will not leave a call feeling inspired and then do nothing for a week. There is work between sessions and I will ask about it.

Not a blueprint

I will not tell you what your life should look like. Buying somebody else’s answer is most likely part of how you got here.

Not fast

Eight weeks gets you direction, reasons and the first real moves. It does not get you a finished life, and anyone promising that is selling you the thing that already failed.

The method

Half deep conversation, half structure.

One. Honest inventory
What is actually wrong

Where you are settling, what drains you, what you are tolerating, and which parts of your life you never actually picked.

Know yourself

When you were happiest, what you do without being paid, where you thrive, what you actually value. We keep asking why.

Recognize patterns

Perfectionism, avoidance, people pleasing, staying too long, refusing to try in case it fails. The behaviours that keep repeating.

Two. Build the capacity
Rebuild self-trust

Stop handing your worth to other people. The goal is not immunity to criticism. It is an internal filter: I hear you, I will consider it, I decide what it means.

Become more capable

Health, money, skills, boundaries, environment, community. Repeated small actions that build evidence and options, not a morning routine that fixes your life.

Three. Test it and move
Pressure-test the dream

Do you want the reality or the picture of it? What does an average Tuesday look like? I challenge the idea to make it stronger, not to talk you out of it.

Build the path

Insight becomes a plan you can actually run. Savings, a skill, a job change, a test, a boundary, a move.

Act, reflect, adjust

There is no perfect decision. Decide with what you know, live it, take the lesson, adjust, keep going. A path that does not work still tells you something thinking never would.

I always pictured a specific life. Nice things, travel, new people, real experiences. What I never had was the how. The picture was vivid and the bridge to it was missing, and that is the exact problem I work on now.

The first thing that changed was a question. I was managing a café, waking before the sun, running on a low hum of stress I had started calling normal. I was the best hand the place had and it was slowly becoming clear that none of it was appreciated. My boss asked me, “Why do you need an assistant manager if I’m paying you?”

A year earlier that would have wrecked me. Instead I said: I don’t know, actually. That’s a really good question. You should find out. You have a month. I leave for Europe soon, and when I come back, I won’t be your manager anymore.

I walked out with a weight off my shoulders I had not known I was carrying. What I understood that day was simple and it took me twenty-six years to get to. Everyone is the main character of their own life. So am I.

What came after was not a straight line. At twenty-one I wanted to model and decided I could not, so I quit right as it started working. For years I read that as irony. Now I read it as mercy. I was not ready, and the years I spent instead running businesses, moving, failing at things, are what made me someone who could hold it. The detour was never a detour. It was the becoming.

I started my first company in my thirty-fourth year and made my first ten thousand dollars in a month. Small against the life I am still building, and the first hard evidence that the path was real and I was on it rather than imagining it.

I am not here because it all worked. I have paid for programmes that were harder than advertised, started things I backed out of, and been burned on things I should have questioned. I am here because I kept going and paid attention, and because most people buying the next answer are trying to purchase clarity that only comes from understanding themselves.

I am not going to tell you what your life should look like. I will help you become someone who can answer that.

Start with the questions

Questions

Is this therapy?

No. I am not a therapist and I do not treat trauma, depression or any mental health condition. Coaching can look at your patterns and where they came from, but the work is forward-facing and practical. If what you need is clinical care, I will say so rather than take your money.

What if I have no idea what I want?

That is the starting point, not a disqualifier. Most people arrive able to describe what is wrong and unable to describe what they want instead. Figuring that out is the first half of the work.

I cannot quit my job or leave my relationship right now. Is this pointless?

No, and this is the part most people get wrong. You do not have to change everything today. You do have to stop using that as a reason to change nothing. Eight weeks of building capacity is how the option exists in six months.

How is this different from the courses I have already bought?

A course sells you somebody else’s answer. If that worked, you would not be reading this. The work here is understanding yourself well enough to generate your own answer, then pressure-testing it before you spend a year on it.

Do you tell me what to do?

No. I ask, challenge, structure and hold you to what you said you wanted. You decide. If I handed you a direction, you would be outsourcing the exact thing we are trying to fix.

What happens if I do not do the work between sessions?

We look at why. Was the goal wrong, was it fear, was it avoidance, was the plan unrealistic, or did life genuinely get in the way. Then we adjust. I am not going to shame you, and I am also not going to pretend it did not happen.

Is this only for women?

No. Most of the people who find me are women, but the qualifier is readiness, not gender. If you are open, willing to be uncomfortable and actually want feedback, this works.

What if what I need is a business, a therapist or a financial planner?

Then that is what I will tell you. I have real business and leadership experience and I will use it where it applies. Where it does not, I would rather point you to the right person than pretend. If I ever earn anything from a referral, you will know.

Will I have my life figured out in eight weeks?

No, and be careful of anyone who says otherwise. You will have direction, reasons, a plan and the beginning of trusting your own judgement. The rest is you living it.

What does it cost and how do I start?

Start with the quiz, or send me a note. If it looks like a fit we will talk, and I will give you the full details and the price then.
